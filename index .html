I didn't start with a cockpit. I started with a dream that felt too big for the room I was standing in.

Outspoken since day one. Extroverted to a fault. The kind of person who feels everything loudly — joy, fear, doubt, hope. I laugh hard, I care harder, and I don't know how to do anything halfway.

I looked at the sky and decided: that's where I'm going.

Mission 01

Origin

I didn't come from a family of officers. I didn't grow up around airfields or uniforms. What I had was a direction — and a stubborn refusal to look away from it.

I am loyal to the people who matter. Outspoken when something needs to be said. Emotional — and I used to think that was a weakness, until I realized it's fuel.

I question myself constantly. And then I do it anyway.

That's where this story starts. Not with achievements. With aim.

Mission 02

Training ground

Right now, I'm building. Not there yet — building.

  • Learning discipline when motivation runs out
  • Showing up when nobody's watching
  • Failing at things I thought I'd be good at
  • Getting back up anyway

Alongside the dream, I'm in the first year of my BCA — learning how technology works while I train myself for the sky.

Some days I'm confident. Some days I'm convinced I'm not enough. Both are true. I keep moving regardless.

This is the unglamorous part. No medals here. Just late nights, small wins, and a stubborn refusal to quit.

Mission 03

The dream

The Air Force isn't just a career goal for me. It's a direction.

It's the thing that makes me wake up early, push harder, and hold myself to a standard I haven't reached yet. It's the reason I care about discipline, loyalty, and showing up even when I'm scared.

I want to wear that uniform not because it looks good — but because of what it means. Honor. Service. Something bigger than me.

Flight lineage

This character is built on the shoulders of real officers who walked this path before.

Air Marshal Prithi Singh

  • Flew about 102 aircraft types and 120+ versions — an IAF record
  • 40 years of service, 6,300+ hours without a single avoidable accident
  • Param Vishisht Seva Medal, Ati Vishisht Seva Medal, Vayu Sena Medal (Bar)

Wing Commander Padmanabha Gautam

  • Six missions in the 1971 war, low-level raids at 100 feet under heavy fire
  • Bar to Maha Vir Chakra for gallantry

Wing Commander Vyomika Singh

  • IAF's first woman helicopter pilot to earn the 'Master Greencard' rating
  • Certified parajumper, mountaineer, sea sailor, scuba diver
  • Scaled Mt Manirang (21,631 ft), won 8 golds in athletics

Group Captain Abhinandan Varthaman
